Cut through the middle.

Printed PLASteelMotor

  • Two discs, half a lobe apart

    The discs wobble in opposite directions on the eccentric shaft, so the shaft stays balanced at speed.

  • Forty-one pins, forty lobes

    Each turn of the motor walks the discs back by one lobe. That is where 40 : 1 comes from.

  • Rollers carry the load out

    Six pins with twelve MR106 rollers pass through the discs and turn the output flange.
